01 / Position

Curiosity is not a brand statement. It is a reason to make something, test it, and find out what happens.

TensoraMax Lab is an independent AI and software research studio. It brings together model experiments, developer tools, digital projects, and open-ended exploration. Some work will succeed. Some will break. Both are worth learning from.
